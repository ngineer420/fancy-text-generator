Strikethrough, Underline & Overline

Formatting for fields that have no formatting•copy it straight in

How is this different from the strikethrough button in an editor?

An editor stores your text plainly and records "draw a line through this" alongside it. That instruction is lost the moment the text leaves the editor. These effects put the line in the text: each character is followed by a combining mark — U+0336 for a strikethrough, U+0332 for an underline, U+0305 for an overline — that the font draws attached to the character before it. Nothing has to support your formatting, because there is no formatting, only characters.

Why does my character count jump?

Because every mark is a character. A ten-letter word with a strikethrough is twenty characters to anything counting, while still looking like ten on screen — which is why the readout above shows both numbers. Stack two effects and it is thirty. This is the single most common surprise with this kind of text, and it is why a bio that looks well short of the limit gets refused.

Which one should I use?

Strikethrough for a crossed-out price or an edit. Underline for emphasis, though it reads as a link to a lot of people. Double underline when a single one is being mistaken for a link. Overline for the vinculum in maths — a mean, or a repeating decimal — which otherwise cannot be written in plain text at all. Slash when you want it unmissable. They stack, and you can chain them further in the Font Combiner.
