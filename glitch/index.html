What is zalgo text made of?

Combining marks. Unicode lets an accent attach to the character before it, and it does not stop at one — stack a dozen and they pile up above and below the letter until the line bleeds into its neighbours. Zalgo is that mechanism used on purpose: the base text is untouched and perfectly readable to a computer, with a heap of U+0300-range marks riding on top. That is also why it copies cleanly into a chat box.

Why does the slider stop getting stronger?

Because text shaping is not linear in the number of marks on one character — the renderer has to position each one relative to everything already stacked — so an uncapped slider is a reliable way to freeze a browser tab. This one caps at twelve marks per character, which is well past unreadable and still lays out instantly. The readout says so when you reach it, rather than leaving the top of the slider feeling broken.

Where can I actually use it?

Discord, most chat apps and anywhere on the web that renders raw text. Expect it to be refused elsewhere: the character count is enormous — watch the readout above — so anything with a length limit will reject it, and several platforms strip combining marks on save. Some also treat tall stacks as abuse, since the marks overflow into the line above. Use it where it is a joke, not where it is a username.
