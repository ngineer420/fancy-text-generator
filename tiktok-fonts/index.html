Fonts for TikTok

18 styles for an 80-character bio•where every character counts

What the app actually does with your text

TikTok's bio is 80 characters. That single number does more to decide what is worth pasting there than font coverage does, and it is why this list is the shortest of the four: every combining-mark style is deliberately absent.

A strikethrough or an underline is not drawn by the app. It is a second character glued to each letter, so an 18-character name becomes 34 characters against the limit with nothing visible to account for the difference. On a 150-character bio that is annoying; on 80 it takes half the field. The styles below all cost exactly what they look like they cost.

Styles that fit the field (18)

One character in, one character out for every style here, so what you see is what the counter charges you. None of them contains a colour-emoji character and none can form a flag.

What is deliberately missing

Anything built from combining marks

Strikethrough, underline, double underline, slashed and overline all double the character count. On an 80-character bio that is the most expensive decoration on the site.

Regional indicator letters

They double the count as well, and any adjacent pair spelling a country code turns into that flag.

Negative squared letters

🅰 🅱 🅾 🅿 carry the emoji property and are drawn from the emoji font, so those four letters arrive in colour.

Greek-style symbol text

Its j is Hebrew and its g is Arabic, both right-to-left characters that reorder the text around them.

Subscript

Unicode has no subscript capitals at all and only seventeen lowercase letters, so most of a name passes through unstyled. It earns its place in a chemical formula, not in a bio.

How this list was checked

Each style was run over the whole alphabet and the output examined directly: the Unicode blocks it uses, whether any character carries the emoji property or is a regional indicator or is right-to-left, and — the one that matters most here — what an 18-character name costs in code points once styled, since that is the unit a bio limit counts. Styles that cost more than they look are excluded rather than listed with a footnote. None of this involved pasting anything into the live app on a rack of test phones; it is a statement about the characters and about the documented size of the field.

The check itself is in the repository as tools/platform_check.js, and test/platform.test.js fails if this page ever lists a style the check rejects — so the table and its evidence cannot quietly drift apart.

TikTok questions

Why is the character count different from what I typed?

Because some styles add characters instead of replacing them. A combining mark is its own character sitting on top of a letter, so a word can look ten characters long and count as twenty. Every style on this page replaces one character with one character, which is why they are the ones listed.

Can I style my TikTok username?

No. The username is restricted to letters, numbers, underscores and periods. The nickname shown above your bio is a separate field and does take styled characters.

Does styled text affect whether my videos get found?

Search matches characters, so a styled word is not the plain word as far as the index is concerned. Keep the words you want found — your niche, your name — in plain letters, and style the decoration around them.

Which styles are safest on an older Android phone?

The ones built from long-established parts of Unicode: small caps, circled letters and fullwidth text. Fullwidth in particular comes from the CJK block, so any phone with Chinese, Japanese or Korean support already has every character.
